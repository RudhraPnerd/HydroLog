# Requirements

> [!WARNING]
> There are many various requirements for this app

## Mac & Xcode

You'll first need a Mac (or Hackintosh) with a software called Xcode installed on your mac. **If you don't have Xcode on your Mac, you can download it from the App Store or at****
**[](developer.apple.com) and download it from there if your Mac (or Hackintosh) is not compatible**

## iPad

An iPad with iOS 16 or newer is required if you want to use the app. If you don't have a iPad running iOS 16 or newer, you cannot download or use the app.
